PAGE 4: PRIVATE CANDIDATE & EXAM GUIDE
1. PAGE HEADER
- Title: Understanding the Private Candidate Pathway
- Subtitle: Transparent Guidance on How Cambridge Board Exams & Qualifications Work
2. WHAT IS AN INDEPENDENT TUITION PLATFORM?
Bethel House of God Academy operates as an independent online tuition and learning support platform. We provide complete daily academic delivery, virtual live teaching, Edubridge platform access, past-paper drills, and progress tracking.
We do not charge high administrative board affiliation surcharges. Instead, our model allows parents to pay purely for quality teaching, while board examination registration is completed independently when the learner is ready.
3. HOW BOARD EXAMINATIONS WORK
- Tuition at BHGA: Learners complete their daily learning, live classes, and revision online with Bethel House of God Academy.
- Selecting an Exam Centre: When ready to write board exams (such as Checkpoints, IGCSE, or AS Levels), parents register their child as a Private Candidate at an accredited open Cambridge examination centre (such as British Council South Africa or approved local open centres).
- Sitting Examinations: The student physically sits for their external board papers during the official May/June or October/November examination series.
- Receiving UK Qualifications: Cambridge International issues official hardcopy certificates directly to the examination centre for collection.
4. SOUTH AFRICAN UNIVERSITY ENTRY (USAF EXEMPTION)
Cambridge qualifications are fully recognized by all South African public universities (such as UCT, Wits, UP, Stellenbosch, Unisa, etc.) and international institutions worldwide.
To qualify for a South African Foreign Matriculation Exemption (Bachelor's Degree Pass equivalent), high school learners complete:
4 AS Levels (Grades A–D) + 1 IGCSE (Grades A*–C)
Once board results are released in January, certificates are submitted to Universities South Africa (USAf) to receive a formal Matriculation Exemption Certificate for direct university enrollment.
